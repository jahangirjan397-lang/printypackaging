## Backlink kya hai?

Jab kisi aur website par aap ki website ka link hota hai, wo **backlink** hai. Google ise ek "vote" samajhta hai. Jitne achhe aur **mauzu se juray** websites se link, utni behtar ranking.

**Aap ki website ki sab se bari kami abhi backlinks hi hain.** Content aur technical SEO achha hai.

## Kya karein, kya na karein

**Karein:**
- Asli business directories, packaging aur printing websites, apne suppliers aur customers
- Har hafte **3-5 naye backlinks**. Aahista aur mustaqil kaam kaam karta hai
- Har backlink ka record **Backlinks Tracker** mein

**Na karein:**
- Fiverr par "1000 backlinks for $5" **kabhi nahi**. Google penalty laga deta hai
- Link khareedna ya link farms
- Har jagah ek jaisa copy-paste text

## Backlink kahan se lein (shuru ke liye list)

**1. Business profiles (pehle mahine, aasaan):**
Google Business Profile, Bing Places, LinkedIn Company Page, Facebook Page, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube, Crunchbase, Trustpilot, Yelp, Clutch.co, GoodFirms, Hotfrog, Brownbook, Manta, Cylex, Europages (Europe), Kompass, ThomasNet (US suppliers)

**2. Packaging aur printing ki jagah:**
- Packaging directories aur marketplaces
- Printing forums
- Packaging design communities (Dribbble, Behance): apne box designs daalein aur profile mein website link

**3. Guest posts (mahine mein 1-2):**
Ecommerce, small business, Shopify, candle-making, cosmetics aur bakery blogs ko email karein aur ek kaam ka article offer karein, jaise "How to choose packaging for your candle brand", jismein ek link aap ki website ka ho.

**4. Customers aur suppliers:**
Customer ki website par "Packaging by Printy Packaging" ka chhota sa link, aur suppliers ki "our partners" list.

**5. Sawal-jawab:**
Reddit (r/smallbusiness, r/packaging), Quora: asli madad karein, spam nahi. Sirf zaroorat ho to link dein.

## Backlinks Tracker kaise use karein

1. **Backlinks Tracker**, phir **+ Backlink**.
2. Website name, type aur status bharein:
   - **Idea**: soch rahe hain
   - **Requested**: request ya email bheja
   - **Live**: link lag gaya (us page ka link zaroor likhein)
   - **Lost**: link hat gaya
3. **Login / contact used** mein sirf ye likhein ke kis email se account banaya. **Password yahan kabhi na likhein.**
4. Har mahine "Live" wale links khol kar check karein ke abhi bhi lage hain.
