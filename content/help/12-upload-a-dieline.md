Har nayi dieline website par apna page banati hai. Is se Google par traffic aata hai aur clients download ke waqt apni email dete hain (lead).

## Qadam

1. Baen menu mein **Dielines** → **+ Dieline** dabayein.
2. **Box style name**: English mein naam, jaise *Double Lock Tray*.
3. **URL slug**: chhote huroof aur dash, jaise `double-lock-tray`. Publish ke baad na badlein.
4. **Date added**: aaj ki tareekh.
5. **Category**: list mein se sahi qisam chunein.
6. **Dieline image**: dieline ki saaf tasveer. White background ho, kam az kam 1000 px chauri, aur us par koi naam, label ya kisi aur brand ka logo na ho.
   - Illustrator / Corel se: sirf dieline select karein, *Export as PNG* (300 dpi).
7. **Downloadable file** (zaroori nahi): asal file jo client download karega. **PDF ya SVG** behtar hai.
8. **Short description**: 2 jumle English mein, box kya hai aur kyun acha hai.
9. **Best for**: 3-4 lines, jaise *Cosmetics*, *Candles*, *Gift sets*.
10. **Publish** → **Publish now**. 2-3 minute baad page `printypackaging.com/dielines/SLUG` par aa jata hai, aur library mein **Newly added** mein sab se upar dikhta hai.

## Achi dieline ke liye

- Har din 8-10 dielines theek hain. Har ek ka naam aur description alag ho, ek jaise text copy na karein.
- Naam wahi likhein jo log Google par dhoondte hain: *Mailer Box*, *Tuck End Box*, *Gable Box*, *Pillow Box*, *Hexagon Box*.
- Food boxes ke liye "food safe" na likhein.
