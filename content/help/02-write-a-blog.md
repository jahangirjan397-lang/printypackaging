## Naya blog – step by step

1. Admin mein **Blogs** kholein, **+ Blog** dabayein.
2. **Title** likhein (50-65 characters). Jaise: *Mailer Box Sizes: A Simple Size Chart for Ecommerce Brands*
3. **URL slug**: chhote English huroof aur dash. Jaise: `mailer-box-size-chart`. Publish ke baad slug na badlein, warna Google par purana link toot jayega.
4. **Publish date**: aaj ki tareekh. Sab se naya blog website par sab se upar aata hai.
5. **Category**, **Read time** (200 alfaaz ≈ 1 min) aur **Short summary** (1-2 jumle) bharein.
6. **Cover image** upload karein (landscape, kam az kam 1200 x 900) aur uski description (alt text) likhein.
7. **SEO keywords**: 5-8 alfaaz jo log Google par likhte hain.
8. **Sections**: har section ka heading aur text. Naya paragraph shuru karne ke liye ek line khaali chhor dein.
9. Table chahiye to **Table** mein likhein. Har line ek row ho, cells ke beech `|` lagayein, aur pehli line heading ho:
   ```
   Size | Good for
   10 x 10 | Small burgers
   12 x 12 | Standard burgers
   ```
10. **FAQs**: 3-5 aam sawal aur jawab.
11. Tayyar na ho to **Hide from website (draft)** on kar ke Publish karein. Blog save hoga lekin website par nahi dikhega.
12. **Publish**, phir 2-3 minute baad printypackaging.com/blog check karein.

## Blog aisa likhein ke insaan ka likha lage

- **Apna tajurba likhein:** "Pichle mahine ek candle brand ne…", "Hum 350 GSM se kam board mailer ke liye mashwara nahi dete kyunke…". Ye kisi AI ke paas nahi hota.
- **Seedhi baat karein:** "Mailer box ki qeemat 3 cheezon par chalti hai: size, quantity aur print."
- **Ye alfaaz na likhein:** elevate, seamless, unlock, game-changer, delve, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "in conclusion".
- **Jumle chhote bhi, lambe bhi** rakhein. Har cheez bullet points mein na likhein.
- **Apni raaye dein:** "Sach kahein to 500 se kam boxes ke liye rigid box lena mehnga padta hai."
- **Asli numbers aur asli photos** daalein.

## SEO checklist (Publish se pehle)

- [ ] Title mein main keyword hai
- [ ] Summary 140-160 characters ki hai
- [ ] Pehle paragraph mein main keyword ek dafa aa gaya
- [ ] 4-8 sections hain, har ek ka saaf heading
- [ ] Kam az kam ek table, list ya chart hai
- [ ] 3-5 FAQs hain
- [ ] Cover image ki description likhi hai
- [ ] Blog kam az kam 800 alfaaz ka hai
- [ ] Aakhir mein quote ya WhatsApp ki taraf ishara hai

## Topics ke ideas

Size charts (mailer, bakery, pizza, gift), "X vs Y" muqable (kraft vs white, matte vs gloss), industry guides (candle, cosmetic, bakery), mausami ideas (Christmas, Eid, Valentine), aur buyer guides (quote kaise lein, artwork kaise bhejein).

Publish ke baad Google Search Console mein us blog ka link **URL inspection** mein daal kar **Request indexing** dabayein. Dekhein: **Weekly routine & SEO**.
