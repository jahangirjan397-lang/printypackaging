## Har hafte (1 ghanta)

| Kaam | Kahan | Kya dekhein |
|---|---|---|
| **Leads** | Google Sheet, Dashboard tab | Kitni leads aayin, kahan se (source), kaunsa product |
| **Search Console** | search.google.com/search-console | **Performance**: clicks aur kin searches par log aaye |
| **Naya blog** | Admin, phir Blogs | 1 blog (Write a blog guide) |
| **Backlinks** | Admin, phir Backlinks Tracker | 3-5 naye (Backlinks guide) |
| **Social** | Instagram, LinkedIn | 2-3 posts. Har post mein website ya blog ka link |

Sheet ka Dashboard naye data se update karna ho to: **Extensions, phir Apps Script**, upar **refreshDashboard** chunein, phir **Run**.

## Naya blog Google par jaldi laana

1. Blog publish karein aur 5 minute baad us ka link copy karein.
2. Search Console mein upar search bar (**URL inspection**) mein link paste karein.
3. "URL is not on Google" aaye to **Request indexing** dabayein.
4. Google roz sirf ~10 requests leta hai. Zaroori pages pehle.

## Har mahine

- **Search Console, phir Pages**: "Not indexed" mein koi nayi ghalti (404, server error)?
- **Search Console, phir Performance**: sab se zyada dikhne wale search terms. Un par naya blog likhein.
- **Backup** (Ownership & security guide).
- **Backlinks Tracker**: "Live" links abhi bhi lage hain?

## SEO ke 3 sutoon

1. **Content**: har hafte kaam ka blog, apne tajurbe ke saath.
2. **Backlinks**: sab se bara farq yahin se aata hai. Aahista, asli aur mustaqil.
3. **Technical**: website mein pehle se theek hai. Bas images chhoti rakhein aur links na torein (slug na badlein).

**Sabr:** SEO ka nateeja 3-6 mahine mein aata hai. Mustaqil kaam sab se zaroori hai.
