Ye sab **Business Info & Social** mein hai.

## Business Info

Yahan ki har baat poori website par ek saath badal jati hai: product pages, quote form, homepage aur style guides.

| Khana | Kahan dikhta hai |
|---|---|
| Minimum order | Product pages, quote form, homepage |
| Production time | Product pages, quote form |
| Quote reply time | Quote form, handwriting wala note, header |
| Sample offer | Product pages, sample kit page |
| Sales phone | Header aur contact page (khaali ho to chhup jata hai) |
| Reviews page link | Reviews section ka "see all reviews" link |

**Zaroori:** wahi likhein jo aap waqai kar sakte hain. Ye customer se wada hai.

## Customer reviews

- **Sirf asli reviews**, asli customers ke, unki ijazat se. Google, Trustpilot ya email se copy karein.
- Pehla review add karte hi website par reviews section aur Google ke liye star rating khud aa jati hai.
- Nakli reviews lagana Google aur US/UK ke qanoon dono ke khilaf hai, aur isse website ko saza mil sakti hai.

## Sales team

Asli log jin se customer baat kar sake: naam, role, email aur photo (optional). Khaali ho to ye section nahi dikhta.

## Social links

Har profile ka **poora link** `https://` ke saath daalein, jaise `https://www.instagram.com/printypackaging`. Jis platform ka link khaali ho, uska icon website par nahi dikhta. Ye links Google ko bhi batate hain ke ye profiles aap ki hain.
